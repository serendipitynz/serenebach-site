Serene Bach v4.0.0-beta.24 has been released.

* Fix: When the data disk is full, failed uploads no longer leave partially written images or thumbnails behind. Logging out no longer bounces you back to the dashboard, and if the server cannot revoke the session, the login page now warns you to check the free space on the data disk.
* Development: Moved to Go 1.27.3 and pinned release builds to it.
* Development: Updated the golang.org/x/image dependency to v0.45.0.
* Development: Updated the modernc.org/sqlite, chi, and golang.org/x dependencies.

Download: [Serene Bach v4.0.0-beta.24](https://github.com/serendipitynz/serenebach/releases/tag/v4.0.0-beta.24)
